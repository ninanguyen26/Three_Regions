Catalog-first (Ngoc, 2026-10-23): Ngoc sends photos; each one gets a row here with its assigned use.
Standing rule: no photo, no photo-beat. A photo that fits nowhere is not used — never force-fit.
Rules for use (DESIGN.md §10): diegetic frames only (texting app / postcard / article); story layer, never gameplay;
sender-POV only — no people as subjects, no named character ever depicted; one warm grade across all.
Ruled out (Ngoc 2026-10-23): domestic interiors — no photos inside anyone's house, ever. Tabletop food shots are food, not interiors.
